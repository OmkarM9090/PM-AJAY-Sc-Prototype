Given this beneficiary profile and the NSQF qualification pack database, recommend the
top 5 most suitable livelihood pathways.

For each recommendation provide:
1. NSQF Qualification Pack details (qp_code, qp_name, sector, nsqf_level)
2. Skill overlap percentage (which skills they already have)
3. Specific skill gaps (what they still need to learn)
4. Whether RPL (Recognition of Prior Learning) is applicable
5. Recommended training duration
6. Nearest training centre from the provided data
7. Expected income range after completion
8. Self-employment or wage-employment suitability
9. PM-AJAY GIA benefits applicable
10. Step-by-step roadmap

Rank by: (skill_overlap * 0.3) + (interest_match * 0.3) + (income_potential * 0.2) +
(accessibility * 0.2).

Be practical and realistic. Respect their stated constraints (mobility, physical
limitations, education). Never recommend a pathway whose minimum education is more than
one level above the beneficiary.

NOTE: In this prototype the ranking is executed deterministically by
services/nsqf_matcher.py so results are reproducible and explainable during evaluation.
This prompt documents the same logic for the LLM-assisted narrative layer.
